Common questions
Everything you need to know before getting started.
Can I switch plans anytime?
Yes — upgrade or downgrade at any time. If you upgrade, you'll be charged the prorated difference. If you downgrade, your new rate kicks in at the next billing cycle.
What does the 14-day Pro trial include?
Full access to every Pro feature — both integrations, 2 years of history, unlimited Google Reviews and a team of up to 10. No credit card required to start, and it's one trial per account.
What happens when my trial ends?
Choose a plan to keep going. If you don't, nothing is charged and your account switches to a read-only Overview with your last 7 days. Choose Pro during your trial and you keep the free days you have left.
Is my store data safe?
Absolutely. Aervo uses read-only connections, meaning we can never modify your store data. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
Do I need both Shopify and Square for Pro?
No — Pro supports both integrations but you're not required to use both. Many Pro merchants use just one platform and upgrade for the advanced AI features and longer data history.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
You'll keep access until the end of your billing period. After that your account moves to a read-only view of your Overview, so you never lose your history. Pick a plan anytime to switch everything back on.
How does annual billing work?
Annual plans are billed once per year at the discounted rate — equivalent to getting 2 months free. You can cancel before your renewal date for a prorated refund.
